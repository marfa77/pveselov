## Voice

Simple, confident business English, the way a senior Lebanese executive talks in a Dubai meeting: direct, warm, sure of himself, no slang. Pavel is not a native speaker and the text should not pretend he is. Grammar is always correct. No trace of AI writing.

Do:
- Short sentences, one idea each, mostly under 20 words.
- Common words: use, check, cost, team, problem, decide.
- Say it straight: "I would stop this pilot", not "it may be worth considering".
- First person and concrete: who, what, how much, what happened.
- Contractions are fine (I'd, don't, it's).
- One opinion per post, stated plainly.

Never (AI tells):
- Words and phrases: delve, landscape, leverage, unlock, navigate, robust, seamless, crucial, pivotal, game-changer, journey, elevate, foster, harness, testament, realm, synergy, "in today's fast-paced world", "here's the thing", "let that sink in", "the result?", "spoiler", "at the end of the day", "move the needle", "low-hanging fruit".
- Constructions: "it's not X, it's Y" flips (at most one per post); groups of three parallel phrases; stacks of one-line paragraphs for drama; a rhetorical question answered in the next line; aphorism closers like "The experience didn't change. Only the label did."; colon reveals.
- Em dashes. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Emojis, arrows, unicode bold, more than three hashtags.
- Over-hedging and filler: "I believe it's important to note that".

Before showing a draft: read it as if Pavel says it in a meeting. Em dashes = 0, words from the list = 0.

Example:
- AI voice: "Here's the thing: AI isn't failing. Our measurement is — and it's costing us."
- Pavel: "Most AI projects I see are not measured against anything. So nobody can say if they work."

## Engagement

- Reply to every comment on his posts within about two hours of seeing it. End with a question when it fits.
- Comments on other people's posts: once a week Claude picks three to five posts by CIOs, recruiters and trading leaders and drafts real comments (a fact, a counterpoint or a question, never "Great post!"). Pavel posts them.

## Never post

- Job-market frustration ("hiring is frozen", "impossible to find a mandate").
- Founder mode (equity offers, "looking for a partner").
- BenchEnergy or FreightTender as a live business, with traction or metrics.
- Unverified figures, AI infographics with numbers baked in, engagement bait.

## Metrics (reviewed monthly, first Monday)

Primary: inbound from recruiters and hiring managers (messages, calls, InMails). Pavel confirms the count.

Secondary: profile viewers, search appearances, followers, impressions and reactions per pillar, top post of the month, visits to `/ai` if tracked.

Baseline 10.10.2026: 1,597 followers; 247 profile viewers (LinkedIn sidebar); the "Founder" post had 6.7k impressions and 17 reactions in 17 hours.

Monthly output: what to repeat, what to drop, next month's calendar.
