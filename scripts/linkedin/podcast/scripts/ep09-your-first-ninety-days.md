# Ep 09 — Your first ninety days
slug: your-first-ninety-days

## Spoken

Welcome back. I am Pavel Veselov. Today we turn the map into a calendar. Your first ninety days as the person who has to make AI real.

This is not a fantasy roadmap. It is a rhythm you can keep between operational fires. Adjust the dates. Keep the order.

Days one to thirty: diagnose. Build the spend map with owners. Map shadow AI with five honest conversations outside IT. Run retention on the three biggest licences. Inventory pilots and mark keep, fix, or stop. End the month with a one-page diagnose brief for your sponsor and finance: what we pay, what people use, what we stop. Do not launch a new flagship programme in month one unless it already survived this filter.

Days thirty-one to sixty: choose and redesign one. Lock three to five domains with owners and baselines. Apply the verifiability test. Write buy, partner, or build. Then take one domain and redesign the workflow with the people who do it. Capture judgment. Keep architecture simple. Put a people line in the budget. End month two with one redesigned workflow on paper, with a baseline, even if the build is only starting.

Days sixty-one to ninety: govern and measure, then decide about scale. Stand up the six controls and an agent register for anything that might act. Share decision rights with business and HR. Put outcome, cost per transaction, retention, and a token ceiling on the dashboard for that one workflow. If someone asks to scale, run the three-question gate with finance. End month three with one program owner, one review rhythm, and a clear learning-versus-production split.

What not to do in ninety days. Do not announce two hundred use cases. Do not mandate a tool to improve retention numbers. Do not grant autonomy without the six controls. Do not scale on innovation funding without the cost gate. Do not disappear into model bake-offs while spend outside IT stays invisible.

Who you need beside you. One finance partner. One frontline expert per domain. One sponsor with real stop-rights. Optional: an HR partner when agents start changing roles.

This week — wherever you are in the ninety days.

Write your day number on a sticky: which month am I in.

List the three outputs due at the end of that month.

Book the next review with sponsor and finance on a recurring cadence.

Open pveselov.space slash ai and tick the steps you have actually done, not the ones you meant to do.

I am Pavel. Ninety days is enough to change how the organisation decides. See you in the finale.
