## Spoken

Welcome back. I am Pavel Veselov. When prevention fails, speed and rehearsal decide the outcome — especially for ransomware and payment fraud.

Phase eight. Respond and recover.

Write the incident response plan you can actually run. Roles: you, CEO, legal, communications, finance, HR, any MSSP. Severity levels. Evidence preservation. When regulators or customers must hear. Who speaks externally. Keep it short plus a contact sheet. Offline copies. Approved by the CEO. Reviewed inside a year. NIST’s incident handling guide is the classic structure; CISA’s ransomware guide adds the practical ransomware steps.

Ransomware-specific runbook. Isolate. Preserve. Identify the strain if useful. Check backup integrity. Decision path on negotiation with legal. Order of restore. Password resets at scale. Who can authorise downtime — agreed before the night it happens. Tabletop it once. CISA’s Stop Ransomware material is written for this exact moment.

Tabletop twice a year. One ransomware scenario. One business email compromise or payment fraud scenario. Include finance and an executive. Capture gaps as tickets. Plans that are never exercised fail under stress.

Restore drills with a stopwatch. Bring a crown-jewel system back from backup into an isolated place. Measure time and data loss. Untested backups are a recurring theme in ransomware post-mortems. At least one full crown-jewel restore test every two quarters with a recorded result.

Communications, legal, and insurance ready. Know notification duties. Keep counsel and insurance contacts offline. Map what your insurance questionnaire already asked — MFA, EDR, backups — to this playbook so a claim is not denied on a control you skipped on paper.

After any real incident or near miss: fix the class, not only the ticket. Root cause, control gap, playbook step to strengthen, dated follow-up. Blameless note to staff when it helps. NIST’s lifecycle includes post-incident activity so you do not repurchase the same breach.

How this feels. People avoid tabletops because they fear looking unready. That is the point. Better to look unready in a conference room than in a press release.

If the IR plan is missing or older than a year, draft ten pages and the contact card. Get a CEO acknowledgment.

Schedule the next tabletop — ransomware or BEC — with finance on the invite.
