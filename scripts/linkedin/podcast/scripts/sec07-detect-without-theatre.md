## Spoken

Hi, Pavel here. Assume prevent will miss something. Detection without a human path is just expensive noise. Phase seven in the written playbook — detect — after you have earned it with identity, endpoints, and backups.

Decide what must be logged. Minimum: identity sign-ins and failures, MFA events, admin changes, EDR, email security, firewall or VPN, cloud audit, crown-jewel application auth. Time synchronised. A matrix: source, destination, retention. Critical sources healthier than ninety percent delivery. CIS audit log management and NIST incident guidance both require usable logs before clever detection engineering.

EDR and identity alerts that someone sees. High-severity endpoint alerts, impossible travel, MFA fatigue, new inbox rules — into a watched channel around the clock or an MSSP with a clear escalation to you. Monthly sample: time to acknowledge criticals. CISA ransomware guidance and the Cybersecurity Framework expect an operating model, not only a console licence.

Map a few detections to ATT&CK on purpose. Pick techniques that matter for your crown jewels — phishing, valid accounts, backup deletion, ransomware behaviours you can see. At least one prevent or detect control each. A one-page coverage map. Gaps with owners. Shared language beats buying three tools that all miss valid accounts.

Threat intel that fits your size. CISA alerts and known-exploited vulnerabilities, vendor advisories for your stack, an industry group if you have one. Weekly thirty-minute triage: act, watch, ignore. Known-exploited items that hit your stack become tickets — automatically or by ritual — not a mailbox graveyard.

Hunt lightly but regularly. Monthly: privileged sign-ins, new admin grants, EDR isolations, backup failures. Quarterly: one deeper path into a crown jewel. Findings become tickets. A hunt that is only a board screenshot is not a hunt.

How this feels. Vendors will sell a full security operations centre story. You may need a partner. You still need the matrix, the on-call path, and the ATT&CK page before the theatre. Detection is phase seven because phases two through five remove the easy losses first.

This week.

Write the logging matrix for the minimum list. Mark what is missing.

Confirm who sees critical EDR and identity alerts after dinner. If the answer is nobody, fix that before new licences.

Draft the top ten ATT&CK techniques for your jewels. Tick prevent or detect for each.

Put a thirty-minute weekly intel triage on the calendar.
