The CIO's AI Playbook

Asked to "do something with AI"? Six questions, in this order.

1
DiagnoseWhat AI do we already pay for, use and quietly abandon?
2
ChooseWhich 3–5 domains produce output we can check cheaply?
3
RedesignWhole workflows, or AI bolted onto old steps?
4
GovernDoes every agent have an owner and controls before autonomy?
5
MeasureWhat does the P&L see, against a non-AI baseline?
6
ScaleDoes one transaction cost less than by hand, at volume?
