# Post: dashboards for a top executive (story formula)

Pillar: Head of IT judgment. Not AI. Planned slot: Tue 20.10.2026, morning Dubai.
Source: Pavel's own story (client-side project lead). No company, title or year by his choice.

---

We built dashboards for a top executive without asking him what he wanted to see. He noticed.

Years ago I led a dashboard project on the client side, in a large industrial group. The user was one of the top managers, one level below the CEO.

We were sure we knew what he needed. The plan was simple: finish, show it in his office, hear "well done". He was a very busy man, so why take his time earlier?

He was not too busy at all. He was really interested and went through every screen. Then he said, kindly but clearly: "Maybe it's time to ask me what I want to see?"

That question changed how I understand the job. The first conversation is about what the person needs to see, not about what we can show.

Two takeaways.

For IT teams: senior people are rarely too busy for something built for them. Ask them early.

For executives: if your IT team only shows up at the end, invite them in week one.

Has a senior person ever surprised you by caring more than you expected?
